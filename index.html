Remora Pilot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This policy describes the data handled by the Remora Pilot mobile app, published by Sentiens (“we”), when you use it to sign in and connect to a Remora device via Bluetooth.

Data we collect

  • Account credentials: your account ID, username, and password, entered when you sign in. This is sent securely to verify your identity and grant you access. Your password is never stored on the device.
  • Device connection data: to securely connect to your Remora device, the app creates and stores a unique security key on your device, kept in your device's built-in secure storage — it never leaves your device unencrypted.
  • Bluetooth data: while connected to a Remora device, the app exchanges the technical information needed to operate it.
  • Permissions: the app requests Bluetooth access, and on Android, location access — required by Android for Bluetooth to function, not used to track your location.

What we don't do

  • We do not display any advertising in the app.
  • We do not use any third-party analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties for commercial purposes.

Data sharing

The information above is only used to connect you to our services and to your Remora device. We do not share it with any third party outside of what's needed to operate the app.

Retention and deletion

Your data remains associated with your account while it is active. To request deletion of your account or data, contact your administrator or write to us at the address below.

Security

All communication with our services is encrypted. Your device's security keys are stored using its built-in secure storage and never leave it unencrypted.

Contact

For any question about this privacy policy: contact@sentiens.fr
